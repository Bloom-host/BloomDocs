Premium+ runs on AMD Ryzen 9 9950X machines (up to 5.7 GHz, DDR5, NVMe in RAID 1) in Singapore. Every Premium+ plan is a fixed size: pick the RAM and the vCPU, disk and backups come with it. Prices are in Philippine pesos; billing also shows a US dollar figure converted nightly.

- Disk is 8 GB of NVMe per 1 GB of RAM on every plan. Backups go up with size: 3 up to 6 GB, 4 for 8 to 12 GB, 5 for 16 to 24 GB, 6 from 32 GB.
- Extra ports and databases have no set limit: add what your server needs, within fair use. Free splits add no extra backups; the backups in the table already cover them.
- Disk is not unlimited. Each plan comes with the disk shown; for more, upgrade to a bigger plan. Servers on older plans keep the storage they have and can ask for more in a Sales Inquiry ticket when they need it.
- Longer terms are cheaper: 5% off for 3 months, 10% off for 6 months, 15% off for 12 months. There is no setup fee.
- 32 GB and larger plans include free server splits (3, 4 or 6) so you can run a proxy, lobby and game servers from one plan. Any plan can add more splits for ₱20 each per month.
- Dedicated-core plans are not offered right now.
- 48 and 64 GB plans are limited and often show out of stock. Open a Sales Inquiry ticket to be told when one opens.
- Promo codes that work right now are on [Current promotions](/billing/current-promotions). With none listed, the term discounts above are the only discounts.
- You can upgrade or downgrade to another Premium+ size at any time from billing.atbphosting.com (Services > your server > **Upgrade** or **Downgrade**). Upgrades are charged pro rata for the rest of the cycle; downgrades are returned as account credit. Switching to Budget+ works the same way from **Downgrade**: your server moves to the Budget+ machines by itself, with your files, and the address changes. See [Upgrading or downgrading](/billing/upgrades-and-downgrades).
