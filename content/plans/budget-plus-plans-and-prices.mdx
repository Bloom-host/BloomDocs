Budget+ is the low-cost line, running on AMD EPYC 7B13 servers in Singapore. It suits small and medium servers that don't need the single-core speed of Premium+. Every Budget+ plan is a fixed size. Prices are in Philippine pesos; billing also shows a US dollar figure converted nightly.

- Disk is 6 GB of NVMe per 1 GB of RAM on every plan (Premium+ gives 8). Backups go up with size: 3 up to 6 GB, 4 for 8 to 12 GB, 5 for 16 to 24 GB, 6 from 32 GB.
- Extra ports and databases have no set limit: add what your server needs, within fair use. Free splits add no extra backups; the backups in the table already cover them.
- Longer terms are cheaper: 5% off for 3 months, 10% off for 6 months, 15% off for 12 months. There is no setup fee.
- 32 GB and larger plans include free server splits (3, 4 or 6).
- Promo codes that work right now are on [Current promotions](/billing/current-promotions).
- Upgrade or downgrade any time from billing (Services > your server > **Upgrade** or **Downgrade**), to another Budget+ size or to Premium+. Picking Premium+ moves your server to the Premium+ machines by itself; your files come with you and the address changes. See [Upgrading or downgrading](/billing/upgrades-and-downgrades).
- No player or plugin caps on Budget+. Normal fair use under the Terms still applies.
- The old Budget Micro plan (ARM) is retired when Budget+ launches. Budget Micro servers that still renew keep that plan's rules: a first-month setup fee (not refunded) and the 10-player / 20-plugin limits.

**Budget+ or Premium+?** Premium+ has faster cores, so it is the better pick for big modpacks, many plugins or 30+ players. Budget+ gives more RAM per peso for lighter servers.
