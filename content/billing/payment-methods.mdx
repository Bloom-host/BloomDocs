---
title: "Payment methods and account credit"
---

Pay at https://billing.atbphosting.com under Invoices, or add credit first under **Account > Credits**. We accept:

- **GCash**. Send from the number saved on your account and it is added automatically, or get an exact amount on the Credits page and send it from any GCash account. See [GCash](/billing/gcash).
- **Maya / PayMaya**. Works like GCash: send from the number saved on your account and it is added automatically, or get an exact amount on the Credits page and send it from any Maya account or a bank.
- **Bank transfer** from any Philippine bank over InstaPay, credited automatically. Enter the last 4 digits of your bank account number (not your card). See [Bank transfer](/billing/bank).
- **PayPal** and **card (Stripe)** at checkout. You can save either one so renewals are charged automatically.
- **Litecoin (LTC)**, when paying an invoice or on the Credits page. You get an exact LTC amount to send from any exchange or wallet; it is credited after the network confirms it. See [Litecoin (crypto)](/billing/crypto).

## Auto and Manual

When you pay an invoice, PayPal, Stripe, GCash, Maya, Bank transfer and Litecoin are listed separately, each with a badge:

- **Auto** (green): your credit is added automatically, usually within a minute.
- **Manual** (yellow): staff confirm it. After paying, open a Billing ticket on [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com) with your receipt and account email.

On the Credits page each method has its own tab, and only a method that staff confirm right now carries a yellow **Manual** tag. A method can switch to Manual for a while, for example if GCash notifications are delayed; the tag always shows the current state.

## Exact amounts

For an exact-amount top-up you choose how many pesos to add, and the Credits page gives you an amount with extra centavos (for example **₱500.37**) and a 15-minute timer. Send exactly that amount before the timer ends and it is matched to your account, whoever sends it. A different amount cannot be matched automatically; open a Billing ticket with your receipt.

## Account credit

- Prices are in Philippine pesos. Billing also shows US dollars, converted nightly; the peso price is what is charged. 1 credit = 1 PHP.
- **Account credit** is paid in advance and is used automatically for your next invoice. Credit has no cash value and cannot be moved to another account.
- Payment not showing? Link your Discord with `/billing link`, then open a Billing ticket with your invoice number and receipt. Once staff add the credit, pay the invoice with **Pay with credits**. See [Paying with GCash](/billing/gcash#paid-but-the-invoice-still-says-waiting-for-payment).
- Venmo, Cash App and sending money directly to staff are not accepted. International customers can use PayPal, a card through Stripe, or LTC.
