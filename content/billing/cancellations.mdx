## How to cancel

1. Download a backup of all your files from the panel.
2. On https://billing.atbphosting.com open **Services**, pick the server and press **Cancel**.
3. Give a reason and pick the **Cancellation Type**:
   - **Cancel at the end of the billing period**: the server keeps running until the last day you already paid for, then it is removed. No renewal invoice is charged.
   - **Cancel immediate**: the server is cancelled and deleted straight away. It cannot be renewed or brought back.
4. Confirm.

Changed your mind about an end-of-period cancellation? Open a **Billing** ticket on our [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com) before the last day.

You can also simply not pay the next invoice. The invoice is created 7 days before the due date, the server is suspended on the due date if it is unpaid, and it is deleted after the grace period. See [renewals, suspension and deletion](/billing/renewals-suspension-and-deletion).

<Callout type="warning">

Once a cancellation is processed or a server is deleted, all server files are gone and cannot be recovered.

</Callout>

## Refunds

Cancelling does not automatically refund you. To ask for money back, use the **Refund** button instead of Cancel. If you are within 3 days of purchase, or think you qualify, read [how to request a refund](/billing/refunds) and the [refund policy](/billing/refund-policy).
