---
title: "Renewals, overdue invoices, suspension and deletion"
---

- A renewal invoice is created 7 days before your due date. If you link your Discord with `/billing link`, the bot also reminds you.
- If you have enough account credit, or a saved PayPal/Stripe payment method, the invoice is paid automatically.
- **Renew early or in advance:** open billing > Services > your server and press **Renew**. Pick how long to renew for (one billing period, or several months at once with the same 5/10/15% term discounts as new orders), enter a coupon code if you have one, and press **Create renewal invoice**. Your due date moves forward as soon as that invoice is paid.
- You can also just pay the renewal invoice before the due date.
- **If the invoice is still unpaid on the due date, the server is suspended** (it stops and you cannot use it in the panel). Billing runs on Canadian Mountain time, so suspensions happen around 2 PM to 3 PM Philippine time on the due date.
- **7 days after the due date, an unpaid server and all its files are permanently deleted.** The suspension email names the deletion date, and a reminder is emailed about a day before. Nothing can be recovered after that, and its backups are deleted too.
- Paying the overdue invoice before deletion unsuspends the server automatically.
- Need your files but cannot renew? While the server is suspended and not yet deleted, open a ticket and staff can open access so you can download your files or a backup. After deletion nothing is left to download.
- Your billing account is not deleted when a server expires; only that server is.
