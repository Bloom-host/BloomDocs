This guide shows how to ask for a refund. For the rules on who qualifies, read the [refund policy](/billing/refund-policy). The live policy is also at [atbphosting.com](https://atbphosting.com/refund-policy).

## Who can get a refund

- **Within 3 days of purchase:** a full refund, as long as the Terms of Service were not broken.
- **After 3 days:** decided case by case. If approved, the refund is prorated for the time you already used, often as account credit.
- **After optimisation or cleanup:** if we optimised your server or removed malware, backdoors or crypto miners from it, the 3-day full refund no longer applies and any refund is case by case.

## How to request a refund

1. Download a backup of your files from the panel (see the [backups guide](/using_the_panel/backups/guide)).
2. On https://billing.atbphosting.com open **Services**, pick the server and press **Refund**.
3. The page shows whether you are inside the 3-day refund period and how much you would get back.
4. Choose where the money goes: **The way I paid** or **My account balance**, then press **Send**.
5. Your server stays up until staff approve the refund. Once it is approved, the server is removed. You can see the status of your request on the same page.

No Refund button, or it says the service cannot be refunded right now? Open a **Billing** ticket on our [Discord](https://discord.atbphosting.com) with your invoice number and billing email.

The old Budget Micro plan's setup fee is not refunded; the Refund page takes it off for you.

<Callout type="warning">

Once a refund is approved or a cancellation is processed, your server and its files are deleted and cannot be recovered. Download a backup first.

</Callout>

Refunds go back to the original payment method where possible, otherwise as account credit.
