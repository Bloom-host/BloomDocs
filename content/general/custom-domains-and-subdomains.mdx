---
title: "Custom domains and free subdomains"
---

- **Free subdomain:** in the panel go to More > Subdomain and create one. It points at your server automatically. Free subdomains are a courtesy: a subdomain used for abuse, impersonation or anything against the Terms is removed.
- **Buying or transferring a domain:** search at https://billing.atbphosting.com/domains. See [Buying, transferring and managing domains](/general/domains) for the steps. Domains are sold for **one year** and are not renewed through us right now (see [Renewals](/general/domains#renewals)). A domain name cannot be changed after it is bought; a different spelling is a new domain.
- Domains are **not refundable** once registered.
- After buying, open a ticket with your server address (IP:port) so staff can set it up. If you bought a domain elsewhere, point it with an A record to your server IP and an SRV record (`_minecraft._tcp`) with your port, or move its nameservers to Cloudflare first.
- Bedrock players still need to enter the port unless your server uses the default Bedrock port.
