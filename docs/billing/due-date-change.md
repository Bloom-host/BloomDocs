## Overview 

This guide explains how to change your billing cycle or due date.

---

## How to Change Your Billing Cycle

You can change your cycle (Example: from Monthly to Quarterly) by opening a [billing ticket](https://billing.bloom.host/supporttickets.php) with your request. You won't be charged nor will you receive credit for unused time, as the change will be applied to your next invoice. 

Keep in mind that Bloom doesn't offer discounts for extended payment periods, except for Bare Metal servers.

---

## Changing Your Due Date

If you wish to change the date in which your invoices become due you can request this via a [billing ticket](https://billing.bloom.host/supporttickets.php) as well. 

You'll be required to pay your pending invoice first (if one exists), and then you'll be invoiced a prorated charge based on the days between your current due date and your new due date. 

Please keep in mind that we might refuse to apply the change if you've requested to move your due date recently.
